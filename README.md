# Revenue-and-Profit-analysis-and-visualization-project
I analyzed 2,707 orders from a European outdoor &amp; fitness gear retailer (17 countries, 5 product categories) and built an interactive dashboard to find out what's driving sales and where profit is leaking.
What I found:

📊 Camping and Hiking generate nearly 60% of revenue
📉 But Camping has the lowest margin (42.8%) while Fitness has the highest (51.2%)
🏷️ Discounts cut gross margin from 49.6% (no discount) to 39.7% (15% off)
🏢 Corporate sales are 28% of revenue but earn thinner margins than other channels
📅 Sales peak in June/July and dip sharply in Q1
My recommendations: tighten discounting on big-ticket items, bundle high-margin accessories with tents and boots, and plan stock around seasonal peaks.

How I built it:

✅ I cleaned the data and ensured that it was ready for analysis and prepared 8 PivotTables for the analysis
✅ Dashboard with slicers and a timeline so you can filter by region, channel and customer type
